# ARCSEC Standards

## Stormwater Intelligence Operating System

**Version:** 2.0 Draft  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Date:** September 1, 2026

ARCSEC 2 is organized into thirteen operating standards.

1. [**Authority**](standards/01-authority.md) — Who may approve work, changes, access, and actions.
2. [**Operating Requirements**](standards/02-operating-requirements.md) — Basic rules systems must follow while performing work.
3. [**Sources and Information**](standards/03-sources-and-information.md) — How sources are selected, reviewed, and used.
4. [**Action Approval**](standards/04-action-approval.md) — When approval is required before an action is performed.
5. [**Data and File Integrity**](standards/05-data-and-file-integrity.md) — Requirements for files, records, versions, and file checks.
6. [**Scope and Limits**](standards/06-scope-and-limits.md) — What the system is allowed to do and when it must stop.
7. [**Errors, Missing Information, and Stop Conditions**](standards/07-errors-missing-information-stop-conditions.md) — How failed work and missing information are handled.
8. [**Logging and Audit**](standards/08-logging-and-audit.md) — What important actions and changes must be recorded.
9. [**Standard Operating Procedure Requirements**](standards/09-sop-requirements.md) — How SOPs are written, reviewed, approved, and used.
10. [**Skill Requirements**](standards/10-skill-requirements.md) — How skills are documented, tested, and used.
11. [**Workflow Requirements**](standards/11-workflow-requirements.md) — How workflows are documented, tested, and changed.
12. [**System Access**](standards/12-system-access.md) — How access, credentials, connections, and permissions are handled.
13. [**Verification and Record History**](standards/13-verification-and-record-history.md) — How completed work is checked and how records are maintained.

All thirteen standards are currently **DRAFT**.

A standard does not become active until it has been reviewed and approved.
