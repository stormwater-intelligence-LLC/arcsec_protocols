Moderate Risk Profile Identified

The integrity of the file system and the resilience of the analytical platform are paramount. The ARCSEC Security Risk Assessment (Protocol #6) confirms that the use of the CSV file format introduces a moderate inherent risk profile due to its ease of human readability and extraction, thereby mandating rigorous adherence to access controls and governance policies. The Anomaly Check (Protocol #3) capability must be continually engaged to monitor for deviations in download velocity, which could signify bot activity, data exfiltration attempts, or unauthorized external scraping. Maintenance of system resilience requires a constant state of preparedness to isolate and audit any asset exhibiting a significant spike in anomaly scoring above the defined threshold.
