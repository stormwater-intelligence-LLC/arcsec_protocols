ARCSEC

Stormwater Intelligence Operating System

Status: ARCSEC 2.0 Draft

Purpose

ARCSEC began as a set of protocols created for systems used by Stormwater Intelligence. The purpose was to make sure the systems followed the rules of the work being performed.

ARCSEC establishes how systems should operate, what should be checked, what should be recorded, and what should not be done without approval.

Core Requirements

  • Follow the requirements that apply to the work.
  • Use the correct source.
  • Do not make up missing information.
  • Do not report work as complete unless it was completed and checked.
  • Do not make important changes without the required approval.
  • Keep records of important actions and changes.
  • Protect files, credentials, and system access.
  • Report failed actions accurately.
  • Follow applicable permit and regulatory requirements when the work is regulated.

All thirteen ARCSEC 2 standards are currently drafts. A draft does not become active until it has been reviewed and approved.
