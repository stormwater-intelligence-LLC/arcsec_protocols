# ARCSEC Standard 11 — Workflow Requirements

**Version:** 2.0 Draft  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Date:** September 1, 2026

## PURPOSE

This standard defines how workflows are documented, tested, and changed.

## REQUIREMENTS

A workflow should clearly state:

- What starts the workflow.
- What steps are performed.
- What systems, files, or data are used.
- What decisions are made during the workflow.
- Where approval is required.
- What happens when a step fails.
- How the final result is checked.
- What record is kept.

A workflow should not skip a required step because another system or tool can bypass it.

Changes to an active workflow should be tested and documented before production use.
