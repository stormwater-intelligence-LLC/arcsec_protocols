# ARCSEC Standard 06 — Scope and Limits

**Version:** 2.0  
**Status:** APPROVED  
**Approval Date:** September 1, 2026

## PURPOSE

This standard defines what the system is allowed to do and when it must stop.

## REQUIREMENTS

The system must stay within the approved purpose, access, and actions for the work.

The system must stop when:

- The requested action is outside the approved scope.
- Required access is not available.
- Required approval is missing.
- The system cannot verify the information needed to continue.
- Continuing would require the system to invent information or bypass a required step.

The system must not expand the scope on its own.
